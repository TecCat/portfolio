Selected work

iKima — Breathe & Calm Stress

iOS · 2026 · Product Lead (solo)

Guided breathing with no streaks, guilt, or FOMO. Privacy-first: no accounts, no cloud sync.

Problem
Adults under sustained stress often feel it in their body first — a racing heart, shallow breath, poor sleep — but most breathing tools fail them exactly when it matters: they require menus and memorised steps mid-panic, give no proof that anything changed, and often demand an account or a wearable.
HMW
How might we help someone with only an iPhone calm down in about a minute — and see their own before/after signal?
Challenge
Deliver real, visible measurement without a watch, a cloud, or medical claims — and monetise without exploiting stressed users.

I owned: positioning, PRD, UX, HealthKit integration, QA, launch, and onboarding and subscription refinement.

Pawlaris — AI Pet-Food Scanner

iOS · 2026 · Product Lead

A pet owner stands in a store aisle holding a bag of food, asking one question: “Is this right for my pet?”

Problem
Ingredient labels are dense, full of jargon, and impossible to decode in the few minutes a shopping decision takes. Allergens are even harder. One ingredient can appear as “chicken,” “poultry meal,” or under a different language, so owners easily miss it. For a pet with known allergies, one miss is a real health risk.
HMW
How might we help owners make a confident, personalized, explainable food decision at the shelf, without asking them to become nutrition experts?
Challenge
Existing reviews and ratings are generic. They ignore the pet’s species, life stage, and allergies, and their scores are black boxes, so owners can’t tell why a food is good or bad.

I owned: requirements and the OCR → API → AI workflow; directed AI coding agents under guardrails; shipped to the App Store.
